This is a **proposed direction**, not an available feature or a delivery commitment. No release date is assigned.

## Operator problem

Resource utilization and provider billing answer different questions; operators need their provenance and timing.

## Current alpha boundary

Monitoring data can describe utilization. Current alpha evidence does not provide verified billing ingestion, allocation or monetary savings. See the [current operator guide](../help/guide.md).

## Proposed capability

Evaluate read-only billing imports and explicit allocation rules with source currency, reporting period and freshness.

## Evidence required

Reconcile imported totals to provider records and test credits, duplicate exports, delayed charges and missing ownership.

## Non-goals

No purchases, resource creation, financial advice or cost-saving promises from local utilization samples.

## Contribution entry

Bring a concrete workflow and a reproducible failure to the [contribution process](../community/contribution.md). Agree the smallest contract and acceptance evidence before implementation. [Return to the roadmap](./index.md).
