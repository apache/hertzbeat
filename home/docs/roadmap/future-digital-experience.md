This is a **proposed direction**, not an available feature or a delivery commitment. No release date is assigned.

## Operator problem

Operators want to understand user-facing failures without collecting more personal data than necessary.

## Current alpha boundary

Website/API monitoring exists, but the alpha does not claim browser/mobile real-user monitoring, session replay or full user-journey analytics. See the [current operator guide](../help/website.md).

## Proposed capability

Explore a narrowly scoped client-experience signal with explicit consent, data minimization and failure semantics.

## Evidence required

Prove consent behavior, redaction, disconnected clients, sample coverage and bounded storage before exposing user-level views.

## Non-goals

No automatic recording of user sessions or inference of user health from a server-only metric.

## Contribution entry

Bring a concrete workflow and a reproducible failure to the [contribution process](../community/contribution.md). Agree the smallest contract and acceptance evidence before implementation. [Return to the roadmap](./index.md).
